About CampusEnroll

Empowering Students Through Practical Learning

CampusEnroll is an interactive course discovery and registration platform built to streamline the elective and professional skill enrollment experience for college students.

Our mission is to simplify higher education course discovery. Students can browse comprehensive course outlines, understand prerequisites, inspect course duration and transparent fee structures, and instantly reserve seats in high-demand technical specializations.

College Practical Assignment Notice

CampusEnroll is an educational demonstration project developed for college practical assessment. It is not an official university service, and student registrations are stored in temporary server memory.

Registration Guidelines

Please review the following requirements before submitting your course registration request:

  • 1
    Select an Available Course

    Choose from the 8 official curriculum tracks loaded dynamically from our API.

  • 2
    Provide Valid College Credentials

    Enter your assigned Student ID, active academic email, and valid contact number.

  • 3
    Receive Instant Registration ID

    Upon submission, the backend generates a unique registration ID for record-keeping.

In-Memory Storage

For this college demonstration, registrations are stored in the Node.js Express server RAM (POST /registrations) and will reset when the server restarts.
