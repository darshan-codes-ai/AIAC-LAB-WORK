About BookNest

Where Every Page Feels Like Home

BookNest is an online bookstore platform engineered to provide readers with an effortless, modern book exploration experience.

Whether you are expanding your programming mastery with Clean Code, diving into transformative financial wisdom with The Psychology of Money, or discovering philosophical wonder in The Alchemist, BookNest is built to make reading accessible, informative, and delightful.

📖

Diverse Catalog

Carefully selected books across Fiction, Computer Science, Finance, and Self-Help.

⚡

Instant Title Search

Fast, client-side title search that responds immediately as you type or press Enter.

🔍

Detailed Book Views

Dedicated details view powered by backend GET /books/:id REST calls.

📱

Responsive Experience

Flawlessly styled for seamless reading and browsing on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
